How to Use Book Look

  1. Type in the search bar. Use the dropdown to search only by Title, Author, or Genre.
  2. Tap a genre chip under the search bar to jump straight into that genre.
  3. Check the Popular shelf on the home page — it changes over time.
  4. Click a book to see its details. Tap a genre tag there to find similar books.
  5. Tap ♡ to add a book to your favorites, then choose a reading status and a category.
  6. On the Favorites page, filter by category or status, and create your own categories.
  7. Without an account, favorites are saved in this browser. Sign in to keep them online on any device.
